Subscriptions & refunds

Updated November 18, 2026

Free

Search, saving, comparing and tracking applications are free. Web searches have shared daily limits.

Pro

Pro costs $19 a month, or $59 for a 4-month season, plus any tax shown at checkout. It includes up to 40 lab briefings and 80 email drafts each calendar month; unused allowance does not carry over. Pro renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel.

Payments, renewal and cancellation

Pro is sold by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record, so Paddle’s buyer terms also apply and your receipt and renewal emails come from Paddle. You can cancel anytime from the link in any Paddle receipt email or by contacting us; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

If Pro isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. After that, payments are not refunded for partial periods, except where the law requires it.

No guarantees

No plan can guarantee a reply from a professor, an internship, a research position or an acceptance.
