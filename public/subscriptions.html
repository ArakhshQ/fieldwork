Gold

Gold costs $19 a month, or $59 for a 4-month season, plus any tax shown at checkout. It includes the AI Studio with these allowances each calendar month: up to 30 lab briefings, 60 email drafts, 15 résumé or essay reviews, 15 application builds, 10 interview practices, 10 recommendation-letter kits, 10 résumé imports and 150 coach messages; unused allowance does not carry over. Gold renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel.

Payments, renewal and cancellation

Gold is sold by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record, so Paddle’s buyer terms also apply and your receipt and renewal emails come from Paddle. You can cancel anytime from the link in any Paddle receipt email or by contacting us; Gold stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

If Gold isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. After that, payments are not refunded for partial periods, except where the law requires it.

No guarantees

No plan can guarantee a reply from a professor, an internship, a research position or an acceptance.
