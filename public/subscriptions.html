Subscriptions & refunds

Updated November 18, 2026

Free today

Fieldwork’s current beta is free. No payment checkout, recurring billing or paid account entitlement is enabled. Searches have shared usage limits; access is not unlimited.

Basic and Pro are planned

Plan descriptions show the intended product direction. Prices, allowances, launch dates and integrations have not been finalized. Viewing a plan or using the preparation preview does not start a subscription or authorize a charge.

Before paid plans launch

Checkout will need to clearly state the price, billing period, included allowances, renewal behavior, cancellation process and applicable refund terms before any purchase. No paid-plan refund policy is currently applicable because Fieldwork does not take payments.

Application assistance

No plan can guarantee an internship, research placement or acceptance. Planned AI assistance will require supported application channels and your review of the application before submission.
