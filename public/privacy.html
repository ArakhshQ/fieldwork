Privacy policy

Updated November 18, 2026

This notice describes the current Fieldwork beta. The project is maintained through ArakhshQ/fieldwork.

What stays on your device

Your profile, bookmarks, application statuses, notes and deadlines are stored in your browser’s local storage. Fieldwork does not currently provide accounts, cloud profile storage or document uploads. Other people using the same browser may be able to see this information.

What is sent when you search

Your search terms and selected preferences are sent to our server and a third-party web-search service to discover relevant pages. When you use “Use my profile,” your selected profile interests, field, location and study level are copied into the search controls; they are sent only when you submit the search. Do not include private identifiers, health information, passwords or confidential documents in searches or notes.

Service providers and technical information

Render hosts the website. Tavily processes search queries and retrieves source pages. Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and browser information according to their own policies. See Render’s privacy policy, Tavily’s privacy policy, and Google’s privacy policy.

The application uses temporary server counters to control usage and does not intentionally log search bodies or store a search history on the server. Hosting and other providers may retain operational logs under their own retention practices. We have not configured advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or a sale of user data.

Retention and controls

Local workspace information remains until you delete it or your browser clears it. In Your profile, “Delete local workspace” removes the profile, saved opportunities and tracker from this browser. Export a copy from Applications first if needed. Clearing local information does not erase independent provider logs or exported copies. No exact provider-log retention period is promised here.

External links and future features

Opening an opportunity sends you to a third-party website. Its own privacy rules apply. Paid subscriptions, AI processing of application materials, accounts and automatic submissions are not active. We will update these disclosures and provide appropriate controls before introducing those data flows.

Questions

For a non-sensitive question about these practices, contact the project maintainer through the project issue tracker. It is public: do not include personal information or documents. A dedicated private support channel is not yet available.
