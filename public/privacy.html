Privacy policy

Updated November 18, 2026

This notice describes the current Fieldwork beta. The project is maintained through ArakhshQ/fieldwork.

If you don’t sign in

Your profile, saved opportunities, application statuses, notes and deadlines are stored in your browser’s local storage. Other people using the same browser may be able to see this information.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, we store your email address and your profile, saved opportunities, applications, notes and deadlines in our database so they sync across your devices. Anything you had saved in that browser before signing in is moved into your account. Database rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change these records. When you sign out, the copy in that browser is removed; your account keeps it.

What is sent when you search

Your search terms and selected preferences are sent to our server and a third-party web-search service to discover relevant pages. When you use “Use my profile,” your selected profile interests, field, location and study level are copied into the search controls; they are sent only when you submit the search. Do not include private identifiers, health information, passwords or confidential documents in searches or notes.

Service providers and technical information

Render hosts the website. Supabase provides sign-in emails and stores account data for signed-in students. Tavily processes search queries and retrieves source pages. Fonts are served from our own server. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and browser information according to their own policies. See Render’s privacy policy, Supabase’s privacy policy, and Tavily’s privacy policy.

The application uses temporary server counters to control usage and does not intentionally log search bodies or store a search history on the server. Hosting and other providers may retain operational logs under their own retention practices. We have not configured advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or a sale of user data.

Retention and controls

Local information remains until you delete it or your browser clears it. Account information remains until you delete it. In Profile, “Delete my workspace” (or “Delete my saved data” when signed in) removes your profile, saved opportunities and applications. To remove your sign-in email entirely, contact the maintainer as described below. Export a copy from Applications first if you want one. Deleting does not erase independent provider logs or copies you exported.

External links and future features

Opening an opportunity sends you to a third-party website. Its own privacy rules apply. Paid subscriptions, AI processing of application materials and automatic submissions are not active. We will update these disclosures and provide appropriate controls before introducing those data flows.

Questions

For a non-sensitive question about these practices, contact the project maintainer through the project issue tracker. It is public: do not include personal information or documents. A dedicated private support channel is not yet available.
