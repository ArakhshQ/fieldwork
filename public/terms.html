Terms of use

Updated November 18, 2026

These terms describe the current free Fieldwork beta, a student opportunity discovery and organization tool.

Using Fieldwork

Use the service for lawful personal opportunity research. Do not attempt to bypass usage limits, interfere with the service, impersonate another person, or submit misleading information. Fieldwork is intended for college students and adult job seekers, not children under 13.

Opportunity information

Listings and excerpts come from third-party sources. Information may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. An availability label is not a guarantee that a position is open or that you qualify. Confirm deadlines, compensation, eligibility and application instructions with the original organization. Fieldwork does not guarantee interviews, admission, employment or funding and is not affiliated with listed organizations unless explicitly stated.

Your workspace

Profiles, saved opportunities and manually entered application information are stored in your browser. They do not sync across devices and may be lost if browser data is cleared. Use the export feature to keep a copy. You are responsible for information you enter and for protecting shared devices.

Pro and AI writing

Pro briefings and email drafts are written by an AI model from public sources and the details you provide. They can contain mistakes. Read and edit every draft before you send it, check any email address on the lab’s own page, and only send what is true about you. You are responsible for what you send. Fieldwork never sends emails or submits applications for you, and no plan guarantees a reply, a position or an acceptance.

Pro includes monthly allowances (currently 30 briefings, 60 emails, 15 résumé or essay reviews, 15 application builds, 10 interview practices, 10 letter kits and 150 coach messages a month). To protect the service, we may pause AI features temporarily, and we may suspend Pro for automated or abusive use, such as mass emailing professors.

Free beta and availability

Core tools are currently free. Searches are subject to shared capacity and usage limits. Features and availability may change. No paid subscription is active. Future charges require a separate, explicit purchase.

Third-party websites

Links take you to independently operated websites with their own terms and privacy practices. Review them before sending personal information or applying.

Updates and questions

We will date updates on this page. For non-sensitive product questions, use the project issue tracker. Do not post personal documents or private information there. These beta terms do not limit rights that cannot lawfully be limited.
